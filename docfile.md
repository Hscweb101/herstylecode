33. Final Developer Brief
HerStyleCode requires a complete direct-to-customer fashion jewellery e-commerce website, not a basic brochure/business website. Customers must be able to browse, search, filter, view, wishlist, cart and purchase products directly from HerStyleCode. The HerStyleCode team must independently manage products, SKUs, inventory, orders, customers, coupons, reviews and website content through an admin panel. Payment, shipping, analytics, SEO, security and ownership requirements must be clearly included in the final quotation.
